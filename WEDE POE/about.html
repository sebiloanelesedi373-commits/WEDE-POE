About KiddiesTime

History

Founded in 2020, KiddiesTime began as a small home-based daycare and has grown into a trusted community hub for early learning.

Mission

KiddiesTime Daycare Centre is dedicated to delivering quality early childhood education and care. We support each child's social, emotional, physical, and cognitive development by creating engaging experiences that spark creativity and love for learning.

Values

Safety, Love, Education, Respect and Fun = SLERF
